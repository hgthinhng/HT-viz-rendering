# Equity Single Stock Analysis

Bài deep dive 1 cổ phiếu: thesis, valuation, catalyst, risk.

## Đặc trưng nội dung
- 1 entity focal (HPG, FPT, MWG, ...).
- Multi-period financial: revenue, margin, ROE history.
- Peer comparison 4-6 stocks cùng sector.
- Valuation: DCF, multiples, scenarios.
- Catalyst timeline + risk identification.

## Chart families thường dùng

**Mở bài:**
- `data_hero` hero stat (price target +35%, EPS surprise, ...).
- `infographic_panel` cover signature.

**Financial history:**
- `line_with_annotations` Wave 8 cho revenue / EPS time series.
- `waterfall` P&L bridge Q-on-Q hoặc Y-on-Y.
- `stat_dashboard` 4-6 KPI overview.

**Valuation:**
- `quadrant_scatter` Wave 8 peer P/B vs ROE.
- `bar_horizontal` peer multi-metric ranking.
- `waterfall` DCF assumption walk.
- `scenario_cards` valuation scenarios (bear/base/bull DCF).

**Catalyst & risk:**
- `path_progression` Wave 9 catalyst timeline.
- `risk_radar` 5-7 risk axis.
- `scenario_matrix` Probability x Impact của catalysts.

**Mechanism / value driver:**
- `flow_bridge` cho earnings driver chain.
- `mechanism_breakdown` business model 3-stage.

**Cuối:**
- `executive_summary_box` Thesis/Catalyst/Risk/Position sizing.

## Composition pattern điển hình

Mở: data_hero PT + infographic_panel valuation summary.
Mid: financial history → peer comparison → scenarios.
Cuối: catalyst timeline + executive_summary_box.

Total ~8-12 viz cho bài 10-15 trang.
