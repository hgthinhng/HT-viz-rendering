He be du phong nghiep vu: 5 bo phan neo 5 dong tien bao hiem

Vat the: mot he thong be chua cong nghiep noi ong dan, mo phong dung co che ke toan cua mot cong ty bao hiem phi nhan tho: phi bao hiem gop vao, boi thuong chay ra, va phan nhuong tai chay sang mot be phu dai dien nha tai bao hiem.

Ba bai tu kiem bat buoc

1. Che het chu, con doc ra cau truc du lieu khong

KET QUA: DAT. Xoa <title>/<desc>, hinh van doc duoc nhu mot SO DO CO CHE cong nghiep chuan: 1 be lon + 1 be nho hon noi voi nhau qua 1 ong cheo, be lon co 1 duong ong lon voi 1 van banh xe o dau vao, va 1 duong ong nho o day chay ra ngoai. Nguoi xem quen so do cong nghiep (bon chua, nha may loc dau, xu ly nuoc) se doc duoc NGAY ca truc "vao/tru/tran/ra" ma khong can chu, day chinh la cau truc dinh luong can kiem trong bai nay.

2. Doi nganh khac, hinh con dung nguyen duoc khong

KET QUA: DAT, KEM 1 GIOI HAN THAT PHAI NEU RO (khong noi chung chung). Cac nhan "phi bao hiem goc", "boi thuong", "nhuong tai bao hiem" la thuat ngu CHI TON TAI trong nganh bao hiem, khong doi duoc sang nganh khac ma van dung nghia. Tuy nhien phai thua nhan mot gioi han that: BAN THAN hinh dang "2 be noi nhau qua ong tran, be lon co dong vao dong ra" khong phai la hinh dang doc quyen cua bao hiem, no CUNG mo ta duoc co che hop von/dong von ngan hang (tien gui vao, cho vay ra, mot phan chuyen nhuong khoan vay cho ngan hang khac qua syndication) neu doi nhan. Diem cuu duoc bai kiem nay khong nam o HINH DANG tank+pipe (von dung duoc cho nhieu co che tich luy) ma nam o hai chi tiet gan chat vao doi tuong: (a) chinh thuat ngu "du phong nghiep vu" trong nganh bao hiem VON LA an du tu "ho chua" duoc chuan hoa boi ke toan bao hiem (khong phai LLM tu nghi ra), va (b) co che "1 CONG TY chuyen giao rui ro cho MOT CONG TY KHAC qua 1 hop dong nhuong tai" la dac thu bao hiem, khac ban chat voi viec ngan hang giu lai rui ro tren chinh bang can doi cua minh du co ban bot khoan vay. Ket luan: DAT nhung o muc "an du co che dung thuat ngu nganh" chu khong dat muc "hinh dang hoc khong the swap", nen khi dung that nen kem nhan chu ro rang (phi bao hiem/boi thuong/nhuong tai), khong nen bo hinh nay dung mot minh khong chu nhu mau logistics/det may/thuy san.

3. Kiem danh sach den chart gia

KET QUA: DAT. Khong gauge (khong kim/mat so cho muc du phong, dung MUC CHAT LONG tren truc thang, cung ho ma bullet chart dung), khong radar, khong pie/donut o dau bang so lieu thi phan.

Khi nao KHONG nen dung an du nay

  • Cong ty bao hiem khong mua tai bao hiem dang ke (giu lai gan het rui ro): be phu gan nhu rong, mat 1 trong 5 bien neo, nen bo hinh be phu va chi dung 4 bien con lai.
  • Bao hiem nhan tho co san pham lien ket dau tu/gia tri hoan lai: co cau du phong that phuc tap hon nhieu (nhieu quy con, tai san doi ung rieng tung hop dong), 1 be don o day la don gian hoa qua muc, de gay hieu lam.
  • Nguoi doc bao cao chua quen thuat ngu "du phong nghiep vu"/"nhuong tai": nen giai thich bang loi truoc, vi nhu bai tu kiem 2 da neu, hinh nay dua vao NHAN CHU de tach khoi co che tank+pipe chung chung, bo hinh dung rieng khong chu se doc sai thanh "dong tien" chung chung.
