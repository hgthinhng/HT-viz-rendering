Vong 09 minh hoa nganh, mau moi 1/3

Nha may may xuat khau: 4 bo phan neo 4 bien so lieu det may

Vat the: mot xuong may xuat khau nhin mo mat truoc, tu kho nguyen lieu toi cua xuat hang. Dung khi bao cao co du lieu cong suat, ton kho, va co cau don hang FOB/CMT cua mot doanh nghiep det may.

Bo phan nao neo bien nao

Bo phan vat theBien so lieu neoDoc the nao
Chieu cao chong cuon vai o kho dau vao Ton kho nguyen vat lieu / ty le NVL tren gia von Chong cao = ton kho day, dat truoc don hang; chong thap = mua vua du, rui ro thieu vai khi don tang dot bien
So tram may "sang" (mau accent) so voi "tat" (mau xam) tren day chuyen Cong suat su dung day chuyen (utilization rate) Mau nay ve 5/6 tram sang = 83% cong suat; 1 tram tat la mot chuyen dang dung do thieu don hoac bao tri
/ Ty le mau kien hang o khu dong goi Co cau doanh thu theo phuong thuc FOB (mau accent) so CMT (mau xam) Mau nay ve 3 kien accent / 2 kien xam = FOB chiem da so, bien gop cao hon vi FOB tinh ca gia nguyen lieu

Ba bai tu kiem bat buoc

1. Che het chu, con doc ra cau truc du lieu khong

KET QUA: DAT. Xoa toan bo <title>/<desc> va nhin lai silhouette: van con doc ngay 4 vung khac chuc nang chi tu hinh dang, khong can chu: (a) mot chong hinh vien thuoc thang, dau tron mot dau = cuon vai, (b) mot day may lien tuc voi cac dau kim nho + 1 mau xam khac mau giua day = mot may dang tat, (c) mot khoi hop xep tang xen ke 2 mau = kien hang phan loai, (d) mot xe tai dang do o cua cong voi 2 khung net dut phia sau = hang doi cho. Ca 4 déu la CAU TRUC (so luong, trang thai bat/tat, ty le mau, do dai hang doi), khong phai chi la trang tri, dung tinh chat cua bai tu kiem nay.

2. Doi nganh khac, hinh con dung nguyen duoc khong

KET QUA: DAT (that bai neu doi nganh). Thu doi nhan sang nganh khac: cuon vai KHONG the doi thanh "cuon giay" hay "cuon thep" ma van giu dung y nghia "ton kho nguyen lieu det may" (hinh dang cu the la vai cuon co dau xoay trung tam, khac hoan toan cuon giay/thep cong nghiep); day may voi dau kim nho + oc chi tren dinh KHONG dung duoc cho day chuyen dong goi hay day chuyen lap rap dien tu (hinh dang dau kim la dac trung rieng cua may cong nghiep); nhan FOB/CMT la thuat ngu RIENG cua nganh gia cong xuat khau det may, khong ton tai o nganh khac. Ba chi tiet nay khien hinh KHONG the dung nguyen xi cho nganh khac chi bang doi nhan, khac voi vi du "hop + mui ten" ma bai tu kiem 2 muon loai bo.

3. Kiem danh sach den chart gia

KET QUA: DAT. Khong co gauge (khong co kim + mat so), khong co radar, khong co pie/donut nao trong hinh. Muc "sang/tat" cua tram may ma hoa bang MAU + VI TRI co dinh (khong phai goc quay hay dien tich), dung huong tiep can bullet-chart ma repo da chon thay gauge. Khong co truc bi cat cut, khong co dual-axis.

Khi nao KHONG nen dung an du nay

  • Doanh nghiep chi lam CMT thuan tuy, khong co don FOB nao: mat 1 trong 4 bien neo (co cau FOB/CMT), chi con 3 bien, nen can nhac doi an du khac hoac bo bot callout do.
  • Bao cao ve nganh soi/det (khau thuong nguon, truoc khi co vai cuon thanh pham): hinh nay bat dau tu vai da det san, khong ve duoc cong doan keo soi/det thoi.
  • Khi so lieu utilization/backlog khong co that, chi mang tinh minh hoa chung chung: dung an du nay ma khong co so that thi 2 trong 4 bo phan (day may, xe tai cho) roi vao dung loai "trang tri khong callout" ma metaphor-table.md da canh bao o muc "KHONG dung khi chi de trang tri".
